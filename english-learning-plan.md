# B1-B2 → Native English: 30 Dakika/Gün Planı

## Hedef
B1-B2 seviyesinden günde 30 dakika çalışarak tam anlamıyla native İngilizce konuşmak: doğru kelime seçimi, günlük hayata uygun yapılar, idiom/slang/jargon ve atasözü kullanımı.

---

## 6 AI Prompt Şablonu (Claude ile Kullan)

### 1. GÜNLÜK DERS OLUŞTURUCU
```
Create a 30-minute English lesson focused on [grammar / speaking / listening comprehension].
My level is B1-B2. Include real-life examples, 3 practice exercises, and a short quiz at the end.
Focus on how native speakers actually say this — not textbook English.
```

### 2. ANLIK KELİME KARTLARI
```
Turn these words into flashcards: [liste].
For each one:
(1) the definition in plain English
(2) a natural sentence a native speaker would actually say
(3) a collocation or phrase it commonly appears in
(4) a memory trick or etymology hint
```

### 3. GERÇEK KONUŞMA MODU
```
Act as a native English speaker (American/British, your choice).
Have a natural conversation with me about [topic].
Don't simplify your language — use idioms, contractions, slang, filler words like natives do.
Correct my mistakes naturally mid-conversation without breaking the flow,
and at the end give me a summary of errors + better alternatives.
```

### 4. GRAMMAR DECODER
```
Explain this English rule: [insert rule/structure].
Use simple examples first, then show how natives bend or break the rule in casual speech.
Highlight the 3 most common mistakes Turkish speakers make with this rule specifically.
```

### 5. PROGRESS EVALUATOR
```
Based on these topics I studied this week: [liste],
create a 10-question mixed quiz.
Include fill-in-the-blank, choose the natural option, and error-correction questions.
Don't show answers until I've completed all 10.
```

### 6. IMMERSION ENGINE
```
Here's a text in Turkish: [paste].
First translate it into natural English the way a native would write it — not word-for-word.
Then quiz me on:
(1) vocabulary choices and why those words specifically
(2) idioms or phrases used
(3) what would sound unnatural if I said it slightly differently
```

## 6 Aylık Aşama Planı

### Faz 1: Temel Akıcılık (1-2. Ay) — "Doğal Ses"
**Hedef:** Türkçe düşünüp çevirmekten çıkmak

- Present perfect vs. past simple (Turkish speaker trap)
- Phrasal verbs gerçek kullanımda (get up, give in, look into…)
- Contractions & reductions: `gonna, wanna, kinda, d'you`
- Filler words: `I mean, you know, kind of, basically, actually`
- **Prompt:** #3 Gerçek Konuşma → günlük konular

### Faz 2: İdyom & Deyim (2-3. Ay) — "Native Gibi Konuşma"
**Hedef:** 100+ aktif idiom, 50 proverb passive recognition

- Body idioms: `cost an arm and a leg, bite the bullet, turn a blind eye`
- American slang: `no cap, it's a vibe, lowkey, deadass, I'm down`
- British slang: `gutted, cheeky, knackered, sorted, dodgy`
- Proverbs + ne zaman kullanılır: `bite off more than you can chew`
- **Prompt:** #1 haftalık temalar idiom kategorilerine göre

### Faz 3: Register & Jargon (3-4. Ay) — "Profesyonel + Teknik"
**Hedef:** Formal/informal/teknik bağlama göre dil değiştirebilmek

- Tech jargon: `pipeline, ship it, greenfield, bandwidth (figurative)`
- Business native: `circle back, move the needle, boil the ocean`
- Email dili: `Just checking in, Heads up, FYI, Loop me in`
- **Prompt:** #6 Immersion Engine → teknik metinler + iş yazışmaları

### Faz 4: Native Refinement (4-6. Ay) — "C1→C2 Geçiş"
**Hedef:** Hatasız değil, doğal hissettiren İngilizce

- Discourse markers: `Having said that, That being said, Mind you`
- Sarcasm, understatement (British), hyperbole (American)
- Shadowing: native speaker ses kayıtlarını birebir taklit
- **Prompt:** Tüm 6 prompt rotation + gerçek içerik (Reddit, podcast transcript)

## Kritik Odak: B1-B2'nin Takıldığı Yerler

| Yanlış | Native Doğrusu |
|--------|---------------|
| `I am agree` | `I agree / Couldn't agree more` |
| `Make a research` | `Do research / conduct research` |
| `very` her yerde | `absolutely, incredibly, ridiculously, dead (Brit.)` |
| `I'm here since 3 hours` | `I've been here for 3 hours` |
| `Are you coming?` sadece | `You're coming, right?` — native tone farkı |
| Passive overuse | `People tend to… / They say that…` |
